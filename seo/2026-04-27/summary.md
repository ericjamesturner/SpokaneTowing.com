## Key Observations

1. **Money keyword "spokane towing" improved** from pos 35.6 → 29.7 with an extra click (3 vs 2). It's now 20 spots from page 1.
2. **"spokane valley towing" remains the #1 missed opportunity** — 498 impressions at position 8.4 (top of page 1!) but **still zero clicks**. Title or meta description on `/towing-spokane-valley` may not match what searchers expect.
3. **Homepage clicks more than doubled** (2 → 5) without a position change — likely from improved title/meta from the April 12 changes.
4. **Airway Heights & Cheney location pages converting** — both gained a click each, position stable on page 1.
5. **The position drop is artificial** — caused by Google now ranking the site for generic, non-geographic queries at positions 50-80. Strip these out and core position is similar to or better than Apr 12.
6. **Mobile CTR improved** from 0.58% → 0.64% — title/meta optimizations working on mobile.
7. **/spokane-towing-services position worsened** (30.1 → 37.8) despite getting more impressions — same broad-query effect, since this is the most generic page.
8. **/long-distance-towing lost a click** (3 → 2) — only true regression in the click data.

## Action Items / Opportunities

1. **#1 priority: fix /towing-spokane-valley CTR.** 498 impressions at pos 8.4 with 0 clicks is the biggest gap on the site. Likely title or meta needs to be more compelling — check what the SERP currently shows for "spokane valley towing" and rewrite to differentiate.
2. **Consider whether "spokane towing services" page (3,352 imp, 0.24% CTR) needs splitting** — its position dropped because it now ranks for generic non-Spokane queries. May benefit from tighter on-page targeting back to Spokane-specific intent.
3. **The April 18 query expansion is likely benign** — wait 2-3 more weeks before reacting. If position stays at 45+ AND core query positions worsen, then there's a real problem.
4. **Long distance towing regression** — lost a click and position is at 52. Worth a quick look at the page.
