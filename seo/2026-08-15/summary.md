## Key Observations

1. **Best month on record with zero changes shipped.** 48 clicks Jul 13 - Aug 13 vs 20 the prior 32 days. July = 40 clicks (2x previous best). August pacing 1.46/day. This looks like accumulated ranking momentum (many pages sat at positions 9-16 in the window) plus seasonal summer demand.
2. **Deer Park is the new top page** — 26 all-time clicks, 1.55% CTR, window position 9.5. "deer park towing" itself sat at position 4.9 and converted 3 of 10 impressions.
3. **Airway Heights woke up**: flat at 5 clicks across three audits, then +9 this month at window position 16.5.
4. **Colville resolved itself.** July flagged it as a 0-click page-1 CTR problem; it took +4 clicks (1% CTR) this month with no title change.
5. **The battery cluster remains the top fixable gap.** /roadside-assistance recovered to +4 clicks, but "roadside battery assistance" + "battery roadside assistance" added ~72 impressions at position ~12 and still have zero clicks all-time (787 impressions combined).
6. **"spokane valley towing" regressed**: window position 11.9 (was 7.3), 228 impressions, 0 new clicks. The page still gained +4 clicks from long-tail. Watch — don't react yet.
7. **/heavy-duty-towing is still the weakest service page**: +1,323 impressions, +1 click, and "heavy duty towing" impressions now land at position ~59.
8. **The generic flood keeps growing and stays cosmetic**: /spokane-towing-services absorbed 11,185 of this month's ~16,900 impressions at position 61 for one click. It is why average position reads 47-54 while every local page sits at 8-26.
9. **/towing-hayden-id is the only page-1 page with zero clicks this month** (266 impressions at position 11.2) — new CTR-gap candidate.
