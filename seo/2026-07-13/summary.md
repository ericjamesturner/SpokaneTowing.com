## Key Observations

1. **The /towing-spokane-valley fix worked.** Clicks 3→10, CTR 0.24%→0.7%, position 17.8→12.9. It's now the top page on the site. "spokane valley towing" itself went from 0 clicks (two audits in a row) to 2, and the page is picking up clicks on anonymized/long-tail Valley queries too.
2. **The same playbook should now go to /roadside-assistance.** The battery cluster ("roadside battery assistance" 118 imp pos 11.9, "battery roadside assistance" 109 imp pos 11.3) sits on page 1-2 with zero clicks, the page did 1,165 impressions with 0 clicks, and its core query is losing impressions. This is the biggest fixable gap, same shape as spokane-valley was.
3. **/towing-colville jumped from position 27 to 9.6** — now page 1 with 204 impressions and 0 clicks. Second CTR-fix candidate.
4. **The generic-query flood is permanent, growing, and cosmetic.** ~12K of 23.4K impressions land on /spokane-towing-services at position 63 for national-intent queries ("towing services", "cheap towing near me"). It halves the site-wide CTR and drags average position to 43.7, but money-keyword positions improved underneath it. Don't chase it.
5. **Real click regressions:** /heavy-duty-towing 3→0 (position flat at 45 — its local heavy-duty queries got diluted by generic "heavy equipment towing" impressions at pos 72), /lockout-flat-tire 4→2, /winch-out-recovery 2→1.
6. **/long-distance-towing impressions collapsed** 110→5. It stopped showing for generic "long distance towing" queries entirely. Given those never converted (position 52 before), this is likely the flood receding from one page rather than a loss.
7. **Homepage keeps compounding:** 5→6 clicks, position 33.9→24.9.
8. **Money positions trending right:** "spokane towing" 35.6 (Apr 12) → 29.7 (Apr 27) → 16.3 now. "towing spokane" 44→33.4. "towing spokane valley" 22.9→15.6.
